# 🔒 Security Fundamentals

## What is Cybersecurity?
Cybersecurity is the practice of protecting 
computers, networks, and data from 
unauthorized access, damage, or attacks.

**Real life example:** Just like you lock 
your house to protect it from thieves — 
cybersecurity protects your digital 
devices and data!

## CIA Triad
The three most important principles 
of cybersecurity:

### Confidentiality
- Only authorized people can access data
- Example: Your bank password is only known to you

### Integrity
- Data should not be changed by unauthorized people
- Example: Your bank balance should only change 
  when YOU make a transaction

### Availability
- Data and systems should be available when needed
- Example: A hospital's system must always be 
  working — lives depend on it!

## Types of Threats

### Malware
- Malicious software designed to harm your system
- Types:
  - **Virus** — Spreads from file to file
  - **Worm** — Spreads across networks automatically
  - **Trojan** — Disguises itself as legitimate software
  - **Ransomware** — Locks your files and demands money

### Phishing
- Fake emails/messages to steal your information
- Example: Fake bank email asking for your password

### Social Engineering
- Manipulating people to reveal confidential info
- Example: Someone calling pretending to be from IT 
  department asking for your password

### Man in the Middle Attack
- Attacker secretly intercepts communication 
  between two parties
- Example: Attacker reading your messages without 
  you knowing

### DDoS Attack
- Distributed Denial of Service
- Flooding a server with too much traffic 
  so it crashes
- Example: Thousands of fake requests sent 
  to a website until it goes down

## Basic Security Practices
- Use strong passwords
- Enable Two Factor Authentication (2FA)
- Keep software updated
- Don't click suspicious links
- Use antivirus software
- Use VPN on public WiFi

## What is a SOC?
SOC stands for **Security Operations Center**
- A team that monitors and protects an 
  organization's network 24/7
- SOC Analysts detect and respond to attacks
- This is the role we are working towards! 🎯
