# 📡 Networking Basics

## What is a Network?
A network means two or more computers/devices 
connected together so they can share data.

**Real life example:** At home, your phone, 
laptop, and TV are all connected to one WiFi 
— that is a network!

## Types of Networks

### LAN (Local Area Network)
- A network in a small area — home, school, office
- Example: School computer lab

### WAN (Wide Area Network)
- A network covering a very large area
- Example: The Internet is a WAN for the whole world

### MAN (Metropolitan Area Network)
- A network covering an entire city
- Example: All ATMs in a city are connected 
  through a MAN

## How does Data Transfer work?
Data travels in small pieces called **packets**.

**Real life example:** Just like a big parcel 
is packed into smaller boxes and sent — 
similarly, data travels in packets!
